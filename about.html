Our Story

Welcome to Masha Catering Service

Masha Catering Service is an event catering business based in Hameem Puram, Melapalayam, Tirunelveli. Founded in August 2026 by Mr. Rasool Mydeen, we began with a simple goal: to serve fresh, hygienic and flavourful food at every celebration, with the care a host would give their own guests.

We are registered with FSSAI (No. 22426282000682) and recognised as a Micro Enterprise under Udyam (UDYAM-TN-18-0107593). Our team of 9 takes care of cooking, setup and serving, and we plan every menu together with the client so it fits the occasion and the budget.
