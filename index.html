About Us

Masha Catering Tirunelveli

Masha Catering Service is a professional event catering business based in Hameem Puram, Melapalayam, Tirunelveli. We believe good food is the heart of every celebration, and we bring that belief to your table with careful preparation and genuine hospitality.

Our team of 9 manages everything from cooking to setup and serving, so you can focus on your guests while we take care of the meal. Every event receives the same attention to freshness, hygiene and presentation.
